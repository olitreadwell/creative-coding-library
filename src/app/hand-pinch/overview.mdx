# Hand Pinch

A sketch you control with your hands: pinch your thumb and index finger to grab a soft blob, move it, or stretch it between two hands.

Learners see how a hand-tracking model turns camera landmarks into gesture detection and physical interaction.

## What you see

- A camera feed showing your hands, with dots marking 21 tracked points per hand.
- A soft, wobbly blob that follows your pinch point.
- The blob stretches when you pinch with both hands and pull them apart.
- Nothing runs until you click "Enable," and nothing is sent outside your browser.

## What it teaches

- **Hand landmarks:** the MediaPipe model returns 21 points per hand, each as an x and y position between 0 and 1.
- **Pinch detection:** the distance between the thumb tip (point 4) and index fingertip (point 8) determines whether the hand is pinching.
- **Linear interpolation (lerp):** the blob follows the pinch point smoothly by moving a fraction of the way toward the target each frame.
- **Privacy by default:** the camera and tracker only start after the user presses a button.

## Try this

- Pinch slowly and watch the blob center ease toward your hand rather than jumping instantly.
- Pinch with both hands and spread them apart. The blob stretches between the two grab points.
- Cover one hand and use just one finger. Notice the blob returns to its resting size when no pinch is detected.
