# Tutorial: Hand Pinch

By the end of this tutorial you will understand how the sketch turns 21 hand landmarks into a grab gesture and drives the blob's position with linear interpolation.

## Predict

The pinch is detected by measuring the distance between two fingertip points. When that distance is below a threshold, the fingers are touching.

The threshold is adjustable with the sensitivity slider. If you raise the sensitivity, do you think pinches register more easily or less easily? Think about what "more sensitive" means for a distance threshold.

## Run

1. Click "Enable" and allow camera access. Wait a moment for the model to load.
2. Hold one hand in front of the camera. You should see 21 dots appear on your hand.
3. Slowly bring your thumb and index finger together until you see the blob start moving.
4. Drag your pinched fingers across the camera frame. Watch the blob ease toward your hand.
5. Pinch with both hands and slowly pull them apart.

## Look closer

- The model uses GPU acceleration if available and falls back to CPU if not. The first load takes a moment because the model files come from a CDN.
- The camera is shown mirrored (like a selfie). To match this, the sketch flips the x coordinate: `canvasX = (1 - point.x) * width`.
- The blob uses `lerp` (linear interpolation): `newPos = old + (target - old) * 0.25`. Each frame it moves 25% of the remaining distance. This creates the smooth follow behavior.
- The blob is drawn as a ring of points with a small sine wobble per point. The wobble frequency differs per blob so it looks alive rather than mechanical.

## Break it

- Try the sensitivity slider at maximum. The pinch should register even when your fingers are still slightly apart.
- Try the sensitivity slider at minimum. You may need to press your fingers firmly together before the blob responds.
- Try a two-hand stretch in a small space. Notice how the blob's size and shape change proportionally to the distance between your hands.
- Hold your hand very close to the camera lens. The tracking points may drift or lose accuracy.

## Recap

The model returns 21 landmarks per hand as x/y positions. A simple distance check between two fingertips detects the pinch. Linear interpolation moves the blob smoothly toward the grab point each frame. That is the entire interaction layer.
