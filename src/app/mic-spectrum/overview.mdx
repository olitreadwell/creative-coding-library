# Mic Spectrum

A live bar chart of your microphone's sound, drawn in real time with bass on the left and treble on the right.

Learners see how the Web Audio API breaks a microphone signal into frequency bands, and how four steps unlock a whole category of audio-reactive work.

## What you see

- A row of colored bars that grow taller when sound is louder in that frequency range.
- Bass frequencies on the left, treble on the right.
- The bars respond immediately when you speak, snap fingers, or play music near the mic.
- Nothing runs until you click "Enable microphone."

## What it teaches

- **FFT (fast Fourier transform):** an algorithm that splits a sound signal into bins, each measuring the loudness of one narrow pitch range.
- **Web Audio graph:** a chain of audio nodes: microphone stream into a source node, then into an analyser node that reads frequency data.
- **getUserMedia:** the browser API that requests microphone access, always prompting the user first.
- **Privacy:** the audio stays in your browser. The stream stops and the mic indicator turns off when you disable it.

## Try this

- Speak slowly and watch which bars move most for your voice.
- Snap fingers close to the microphone and watch how the treble bars spike briefly.
- Slide "Gain" up if the bars are too short, or down if they hit the ceiling.
