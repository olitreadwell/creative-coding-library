## What this is

Pointer Flow scatters a field of small dots across the canvas. Each dot watches
where your cursor is and either drifts toward it or pushes away, depending on the
mode you pick. When you click, a pulse spreads outward and scatters every dot
near it.

It looks alive and organic, but it is built from a few simple rules applied to
every dot on every frame.

## The big idea in plain words

A particle system is just a list of objects, each with a position and a speed.
On every frame you do three things to each object:

1. Add up the forces acting on it (here: the pull or push toward your cursor,
   plus any shockwave from a recent click).
2. Change its speed by those forces, then trim the speed a little so nothing runs
   away forever. That trim is friction.
3. Move it by its speed times the time since the last frame.

Mouse, touch, and stylus all arrive through the same browser pointer events, so
the sketch works on a phone or tablet with no extra code.

## Why it matters

Interaction makes a sketch feel alive in a way that pure animation cannot. The
attract and repel pattern here is the same idea behind flocking birds, magnetic
field lines, and the way network graphs settle into a shape. Learn it once and
you can reuse it everywhere.
