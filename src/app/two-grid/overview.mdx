# Vector Grid

A 10 by 10 grid of squares that spin, pulse, and shift color in a ripple wave from the center, using the two.js vector graphics library.

Learners see how a scene graph lets you update shapes by changing properties rather than redrawing from scratch.

## What you see

- A grid of colored squares, each spinning and pulsing.
- A ripple wave starts at the center and travels outward continuously.
- Squares near the center rotate faster and pulse at a different time than outer squares.
- Colors shift with the wave, from blue-purple at the center to teal at the edges.

## What it teaches

- **Scene graph:** two.js keeps a list of all shapes internally. You update each shape's rotation or scale property and two.js handles redrawing.
- **Phase offset:** each square's distance from the center delays its animation, producing the visible wave.
- **Transforms:** rotation and scale are applied around each shape's own center. You change one number; the library handles the math.
- **Euclidean distance:** the distance from each square to the center is computed with `sqrt(dx^2 + dy^2)` and normalized to the range [0, 1].

## Try this

- Watch the center square carefully. It rotates at the slowest speed but pulses first.
- Watch a corner square. It rotates faster and its pulse lags behind the center.
- Notice how the color of each square changes in sync with its size pulse.
