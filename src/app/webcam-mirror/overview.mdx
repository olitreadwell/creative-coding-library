# Webcam Mirror

Your live camera feed redrawn as a grid of colored dots sized by brightness, creating a live halftone effect.

Learners see the core three-step camera pipeline: request access, sample the video into a small canvas, read pixels back.

## What you see

- A grid of colored dots replacing your camera image in real time.
- Bright areas produce large dots. Dark areas produce small or invisible dots.
- Dot colors run from deep blue (dark) to bright yellow (bright) on a colorblind-safe ramp.
- Nothing runs until you click "Enable camera."

## What it teaches

- **getUserMedia:** the browser API for requesting camera access. Permission is always required.
- **Luminance:** a single brightness value computed from red, green, and blue using weighted addition. Green contributes most because human eyes are most sensitive to green.
- **Dot radius from brightness:** a bright cell (luminance near 1) gets the largest dot. A dark cell (luminance near 0) gets a near-invisible dot.
- **Mirroring:** the canvas is flipped horizontally so the result reads as a mirror, not a camera facing you.

## Try this

- Hold your hand in front of a bright background and watch the dot sizes create a clear silhouette.
- Raise "Contrast" and watch dark areas vanish while bright areas saturate.
- Try the sketch in a dark room with one light source to see the gradient clearly.
